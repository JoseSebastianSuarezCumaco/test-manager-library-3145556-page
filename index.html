Manager Library

A system for a small library to manage its book catalog, its members and its loans, and to see which books are borrowed the most.

What it covers

Book catalog

Books with an optional cover, five fixed categories, search by title or author, and soft delete.

Members

People who borrow books. They are data, not system users, and they are never deleted.

Loans

One member per loan, up to 3 books, due 14 days after the loan, with the book data frozen at that moment.

Returns

Each book is returned on its own line, and the loan closes when every line is back.

Overdue books

A list of the books not returned in time, with the days of delay.

Top borrowed report

The most borrowed books in a date range, available only to the administrator.

Who uses it

  • Administrator: manages books and members, creates librarians and sees every report.
  • Librarian: registers loans and returns, and checks the catalog, members and overdue books.

How it is built

The project follows Spec-Driven Development: a Laravel backend with a hexagonal architecture, a React frontend and a PostgreSQL database, all derived from one written specification.
